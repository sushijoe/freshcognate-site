Learning starts with a story.

We design the strategy, engineer the software, and tell the stories that make learning stick.

FreshCognate is a boutique learning experience and educational software company. We apply a holistic approach to organizational development by combining deep instructional design with expert product engineering. Our team of educators, software engineers, and media producers are lifelong tinkerers working to reimagine learning in today's digital age.

We help organizations solve complex problems through custom e-learning content and bespoke educational platforms designed around behavioral change. From partner certifications to product training, we partner with you to develop a winning strategy, build and iterate the right technical solutions, and drive measurable business success.

Our Services

Let us help you craft and execute your vision

Product

We build learning products that put human connection at the center, designing solutions that use technology to enhance—not replace—the irreplaceable human elements of education.

Engineering

Our engineers craft systems centered on human connection and educational effectiveness, while seamlessly integrating AI capabilities where appropriate. Our tools are meant to ease and automate tasks while preserving space for meaningful learning experiences.

Instructional Design

We design multimodal, interactive learning experiences rooted in proven pedagogy. Through thoughtful sequencing and compelling storytelling, we keep learners deeply engaged every step of the way.

AI Orchestration

We're passionate about AI that amplifies human potential in learning environments. We create intelligent systems that provide personalized feedback, develop data-driven insights to identify learning gaps, and build adaptive experiences that respond to individual needs while keeping human expertise at the forefront.

Strategy + Scale

We partner with organizations ready to reimagine learning in the digital age. Our collaborative approach combines educational research with practical implementation, helping teams develop learning solutions that support human connection, and deliver on business objectives through thoughtful innovation and long-term partnership.

Video Production

Our team has decades of experience in storytelling for social impact in education, public health and technology. We produce cinematic documentary films and dynamic educational videos that inform, engage and inspire.
