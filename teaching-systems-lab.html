MIT Teaching Systems Lab

Long-term partnership: Video Production, Strategy and Instructional Design

Details

Client: MIT Teaching Systems Laboratory

Date: 5 September 2015 to present

Online: Teaching Systems Lab

The MIT Teaching Systems Lab (TSL) is dedicated to researching the future of teacher learning, and produces resources to support teachers in their journey. FreshCognate's team has collaborated with MIT/TSL for many years to produce content that supports, engages and inspires educators. Our primary focus is to produce compelling videos, from interviews, instructional to documentary stories from the field that provide real life examples for teachers. Our videos are featured in TSL's MOOCs (massive online courses). We also support course production with strategy, art direction and instructional design.

Check out one of our favorite courses, Becoming a More Equitable Educator: Mindsets and Practices.
